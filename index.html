Why This Matters

Labels reduce harm. Cycles close loops.

Household consumption drives ~65% of global greenhouse gas emissions. Two levers matter most for individuals: buying less and buying certified. A third idea — life-cycle thinking — is how designers and policymakers measure that impact.

Editorial Note

“Waste equals food.”

In nature there is no landfill — every output feeds another cycle. Life-cycle thinking borrows that logic: design products so technical materials circulate and biological materials safely return to soil. The rest of this site shows how consumers, labels and designers each play a part.

— William McDonough & Michael Braungart, Cradle to Cradle
